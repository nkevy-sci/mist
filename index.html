What is Instructional Science?

Instructional Science and Technology brings together research on how people learn with the principles of systematic design. It uses this lens to identify organizational needs, design and develop potential solutions, and evaluate what works and how well.

At California State University Monterey Bay, the Master of Science in Instructional Science and Technology (MIST) program connects instructional design, educational technology, and training. Participating in MIST program was absolutely terrific. My portfolio reflects on my studies, showing how I applied the science of learning to solve problems both in the classroom and the real world.
