IST524

Reflecting on Instruction Design

My final exam examined the foundations of instructional science and technology, including the field’s history, influential theorists, and commitment to professional ethics. I reflected upon my knowledge of systematic design models such as ADDIE and SAM, emphasizing how important an initial needs assessment is for achieving organizational goals. The exam also touched on Universal Design for Learning, designing for accessibility, multimedia learning, technology selection, human performance improvement, change management, and program evaluation. The exam included short essays, one of which asked me to apply some of the aforementioned principles to my proposed capstone project. I explained why it was important to leverage the Canvas learning management system, and I pondered the integration of gamification techniques. In short, the test assessed my ability to think critically about inclusive, effective, and ethically responsible learning design.
