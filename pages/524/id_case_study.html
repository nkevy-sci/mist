The case of Victoria March

This case study begins at Wexford University, a fictional research institution. A group of instructional designers will host a project named Integrating Disciplines for Blood Related Cancers (IDBRC), gathering members of three different schools together in order to spread innovative cancer research and treatments. By reviewing this fictional case study from the ID Case Book, I hoped to learn more about the management of a complicated project. The case provided insights into managing both personnel and resources.

Reference

Morgan, V., & Tracey, M. W. (2024). Victoria March Tackling Complex Content and Managing SMEs. In Ertmer, P. A., Glazewski, K., Koehler, A. A., & Stefaniak, J. E. (Eds.). The ID Case Book (Chapter 13). Routledge.
