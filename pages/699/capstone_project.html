Instructional Design Models.

There are multiple instructional design models that provide guidance for every phase of a project or initiative. ADDIE organizes the design process into five phases: analysis, design, development, implementation and evaluation. The Successive Approximation Model (SAM) emphasizes short, repeated cycles of design, prototyping and feedback to refine learning experiences. The Dick and Carey model takes a systems approach, connecting instructional goals, learner and context analysis, performance objectives, assessments, instructional strategies, and evaluation. These models offer different ways to organize the work of developing and improving instruction.
